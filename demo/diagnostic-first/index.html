× NOT A FIRST PROJECT

I will slow down or decline work involving…

  • Patient, payroll, legal, or highly sensitive records
  • Unsupervised sending, spending, or account changes
  • Production access before the workflow is understood
  • A company-wide “AI transformation” with no first use case

Sensitive work may require a different scope, safeguards, or specialist—not a faster sales pitch.

FAIR QUESTIONS

What owners usually ask first.

What access do you need?+

Only what is necessary to observe the agreed workflow. We define the boundary before the week begins. Sensitive or regulated access can change the scope—or make the workflow a no-go.

Are you building software during the diagnostic?+

Not by default. The diagnostic is a decision product. Small reversible experiments may help validate an assumption, but a production build requires a separate scope after the verdict.

What happens after Friday?+

You keep the written verdict and can act on it yourself, hire someone else, or ask Mote Ops to scope the next step. There is no automatic subscription or implementation commitment.

Why not just ask ChatGPT what to automate?+

ChatGPT can generate ideas. It cannot know your actual volume, exception rate, authority boundaries, staff habits, or where information gets lost without someone carefully observing the work.

What if the answer is “don’t automate it”?+

Then the diagnostic did its job. You avoid a larger bad investment and leave with a simpler recommendation, the supporting evidence, and a clearer operating picture.
