WHAT'S ALREADY WORKING IN YOUR TRADE

Real tools, running in shops like yours today.

WHAT'S POSSIBLE Examples of what current tools do in your trade, not Mote Ops client results. The numbers come from the linked vendors and surveys.

  • Missed-call textback

    The moment a call rings out, the caller gets a text and the booking starts. Industry call data puts unanswered calls at about 27% for small service shops; one HVAC shop reported over $17,000 recovered in its first month after adding answer-and-text-back.

  • A phone that answers itself, nights and weekends

    An answering agent handles service-area and pricing questions and books jobs straight into your scheduling software. Housecall Pro now ships this built in; Avoca builds it specifically for heating, cooling, plumbing, and electrical shops.

  • Follow-up on unsold estimates

    ServiceTitan tracks every estimate sitting in unsold status and triggers a follow-up sequence after a delay you set. No one in the office has to keep the list.

  • Seasonal maintenance reminders

    Past customers get a tune-up reminder timed to their equipment and the season. Vendors report booking rates of 28 to 34%, roughly six times cold marketing, because the message lands on people who already know you.

  • Review requests and drafted replies

    After each job the customer gets a review request, and incoming reviews get a drafted reply for you to approve. Trades companies at 4.5+ stars get about 70% more clicks in local search.
