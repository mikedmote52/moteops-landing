WHAT'S ALREADY WORKING IN YOUR BUSINESS

Real tools, running in restaurants today.

WHAT'S POSSIBLE Examples of what current tools do in your business, not Mote Ops client results. The numbers come from the linked vendors and surveys.

  • A phone that survives the dinner rush

    Phone agents built for restaurants take reservations, answer common questions, and can take orders into the POS. One family-owned Italian restaurant in Chicago cut missed calls 35%, handling calls in two languages.

  • No-show reminder texts

    Automated confirmation and reminder texts cut no-show rates from a typical 12 to 18% down to roughly 4 to 7%. Resy restaurants average 2.9%, and Resy's screening cut bot- and broker-caused no-shows 90% year over year.

  • Prep lists and ordering from your own numbers

    Operators using forecast tools report 96 to 97% sales-forecast accuracy; Chipotle cut waste about 30% with predictive ordering. US restaurants lose over $25 billion a year to preventable food waste.

  • Schedules that match how busy you'll actually be

    Scheduling software forecasts each shift's sales from your history, local events, and weather, then builds the schedule to match. Users report labor costs down around 15%.

  • Review replies drafted same-day

    A bad night's 2-star review gets a drafted response in your voice for you to approve, instead of sitting unanswered for a month.
