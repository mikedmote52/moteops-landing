WHAT'S ALREADY WORKING IN YOUR BUSINESS

Real tools, running in teaching businesses today.

WHAT'S POSSIBLE Examples of what current tools do in your business, not Mote Ops client results. The numbers come from the linked vendors and surveys.

  • Instant replies to new-student inquiries

    When a parent fills your contact form at night, an automated reply answers immediately, asks the qualifying questions, and offers consultation times, instead of the inquiry cooling off overnight.

  • Reminders that stop no-shows and chase invoices

    Tutoring-specific software from about $20 a month sends lesson reminders by text and follows up on unpaid invoices, so you're not texting parents at 9pm or awkwardly asking to be paid.

  • Lesson plans and worksheets you edit, not write

    Describe the student and topic; get a draft plan or differentiated worksheet at the right level. A 2025 Gallup survey found 60% of K-12 teachers already use AI, most commonly for exactly this prep. One honest caveat: research finds unedited AI lesson plans are weaker at inspiring students. Your edit is the product.

  • Custom practice problems between sessions

    Instead of hunting for a worksheet that matches what you covered Tuesday, you generate one at the exact topic and level in a minute or two.

  • Parent progress reports from your session notes

    Software turns running notes and quiz results into the polished monthly report. One vendor documents the cycle dropping from about 4 hours to under 20 minutes.
