WHAT'S ALREADY WORKING IN YOUR BUSINESS

Real tools, running in salons today.

WHAT'S POSSIBLE Examples of what current tools do in your business, not Mote Ops client results. The numbers come from the linked vendors and surveys.

  • Missed-call textback

    The moment a call rings out mid-cut, the caller gets a booking text. Industry write-ups cite 67% of voicemail callers moving on to the next salon and 85% never leaving a message; this catches them before they dial.

  • No-show texts that cut empty chairs

    One-tap confirm, reschedule, or cancel a day or two ahead. Vendors report automated reminders cutting no-shows by well over half; for a salon losing $30k a year to no-shows, that's real chair time back.

  • Booking that works after close

    Zenoti's 2025 industry report found 46% of salon bookings happen outside operating hours. If you only book when the shop is open, you're invisible for nearly half your demand.

  • Same-day cancellations refill themselves

    A cancel triggers texts to your waitlist and overdue regulars, and the slot often refills in minutes. Zenoti's 2025 data puts recovery at 35 to 45% of otherwise lost revenue.

  • Rebooking nudges for overdue clients

    The six-week color client hits week nine and gets a friendly touch-up text. Salons using proactive rebooking see 18 to 24% higher rebooking rates than confirmation-only shops.
