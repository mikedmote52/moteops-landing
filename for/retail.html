Real tools, running in independent shops today.

WHAT'S POSSIBLE Examples of what current tools do in your business, not Mote Ops client results. The numbers come from the linked vendors and surveys.

  • Reorder alerts from what actually sold

    The point of sale watches sales history, seasonality, and lead times, then pings you before a bestseller runs out, with a suggested quantity. Vendors report up to 30% less overstock and up to 50% fewer stockouts.

  • Product listings without a copywriter

    Shopify Magic writes descriptions in your store's voice, removes photo backgrounds in a click, and generates lifestyle shots from a prompt. The difference between listing today and listing Friday.

  • A dashboard you can ask plain questions

    Square AI sits inside the dashboard and answers "what sold best last weekend?" in ordinary words, folding in weather and local events. No spreadsheet exports, no formulas.

  • Email that targets who's actually gone quiet

    Klaviyo builds segments from purchase behavior and drafts the email. One pet-gear brand drove $6.5k in extra revenue in the first month after redoing segments on purchase data. The drafts are a starting point, not your finished voice.

  • Review replies drafted for you

    A new Google review gets a drafted response for you to approve. Shops using drafting tools hit near-total response rates versus about 41% replying manually.
