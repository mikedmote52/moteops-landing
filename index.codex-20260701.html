Method

Diagnose. Map. Adapt. Install. Teach. Expand.

This is the real Mote Ops model. It keeps the work grounded in the business, protects trust, and prevents AI installs from becoming brittle demos.

01

Diagnose

Find the repeated owner burden, the communication drag, the coordination waste, and the review risk before promising a solution.

02

Map

Trace what is happening now across people, tools, inboxes, files, approvals, and handoffs so the install matches the business.

03

Adapt

Use reusable internal systems, then reshape the trust rules, logic, and workflow expression to fit the client’s reality.

04

Install

Deploy a working layer that handles useful work immediately and makes approval boundaries obvious instead of hidden.

05

Teach

Show the owner and team how to use the system, how to evaluate it, and how to keep judgment in the loop where it belongs.

06

Expand

Once the first system is stable and trusted, extend into adjacent workflows such as routing, intake, reporting, or private AI ownership.
