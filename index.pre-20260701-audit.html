What we actually do

Three ways we help make AI useful.

The work changes depending on the business, but it usually lands in one of these three buckets.

Build the front door

We create websites and brand surfaces that make the business feel real, modern, and aligned with the founder’s vision.

  • High-end visual design
  • Founder-owned front doors
  • Conversion and trust clarity

Install the operating layer

We turn messy follow-up, intake, staffing, admin, communication, and reporting work into cleaner systems with AI in the loop.

  • Workflow design
  • Summaries, follow-up, and queue logic
  • AI-assisted manager and owner systems

Teach people how to use AI

We help clients understand the stack itself, so they can keep building and adapting instead of depending on a black box forever.

  • Practical tool and subscription guidance
  • How to think with AI, not just click it
  • Long-term founder and owner capability

Help people own local AI

When privacy, ownership, resilience, cost control, or model access matter, we can help clients stand up local AI systems they control themselves.

  • Local-model setup and guidance
  • Self-hosted AI options for the right client
  • Less dependence on shifting platform access
