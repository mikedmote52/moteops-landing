Mobile veterinary medicine.

A two-sided platform for a Denver-metro mobile rehabilitation practice. Front end for pet families to book at-home appointments. How this works  ·  Free call, scoped quote, build and teach

No tiers. No price card. No subscription. The engagement begins with thirty free minutes and ends with you running the system without me, usually inside three months. That is the entire point.

i. Discovery

Thirty minutes. Free.

Paste in your business context. We look at where AI could meaningfully help and walk through one or two of the live systems closest to your work. If it does not pencil out, I will tell you on the call.

ii. Quote

A scope, a number, the math.

By end of call, or the next day, you get a specific scope and a specific dollar figure. Sized to what the system saves you in time, money, or opportunity. No mystery pricing, no monthly minimum.

iii. Build & teach

v1 in one to three weeks.

A working system, deployed and yours. Then a walkthrough so you can extend it with a current AI assistant on your own. Optional ongoing help is available client by client. Not required, and never the default.
