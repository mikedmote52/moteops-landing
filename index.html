Common questions

Asked before you ask.

What every owner wants to know before booking 30 minutes.

What does it actually cost?
No fixed price card. The free 30-min call comes first. By the end of it (or the next day), you get a specific scope and a specific dollar figure — sized to what the system saves you in time, money, or opportunity. If it doesn't pencil out, I'll tell you on the call.
What if I'm not technical?
Most of my clients aren't. Cindi runs a childcare center; she's not a developer. The systems I build are operator-friendly by design — you click buttons, I write code. After delivery, I walk you through running it with Claude on your own.
Will AI replace my job or my team?
I don't build systems that act unsupervised on anything customer-facing. The pattern across every project: AI drafts, you approve. Your judgment stays in the loop where it matters. What gets automated is the repetitive admin nobody enjoys doing anyway.
How long does it take?
A working v1 usually lands in one to three weeks after scope is signed. You get to use it immediately while I refine. The teaching walkthrough comes once you've had real hands on it.
What happens to my data?
Your data stays in your accounts — your email, your Stripe, your Calendly. I build connectors, not a separate database. Nothing leaves your stack to my servers unless you specifically want it to. Compliance-aware where it matters.
