## Accepted qualities

## Rejection reason

Multiple moving monitors contain generated interface detail. The printer also exposes a manufacturer-style mark. Because the camera moves across these surfaces, a static replacement or defocus would either miss artifacts or visibly damage Mike and the office action. The plate cannot ship under the approved no-generated-UI and no-logo rule.

## One-variable replacement

Change only the hardware and screen staging:

- keep the office, performance, pressure, wardrobe, colleague, paperwork, camera urgency, and final discovery beat;
- show one laptop only;
- keep its display fully hidden until the final two seconds;
- reveal a single large blank matte-green screen for replacement;
- crop the printer so only an unbranded paper-output tray is visible;
- keep every other device screen face-down or facing away.

The replacement was preflighted at 72 credits. Mike then increased the total authorization from 216 to 288 credits before submission.
